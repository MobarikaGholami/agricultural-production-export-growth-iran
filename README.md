# Production Growth and Agricultural Export Growth in Iran

## Project Overview

This project is based on my MSc thesis in Agricultural Economics at the University of Tehran, Iran. It examines the relationship between production growth and export growth for selected agricultural products in Iran.

The research investigates whether growth in domestic agricultural production is associated with subsequent growth in agricultural exports and explores the dynamics of this relationship using time-series econometric methods.

## Research Objectives

The main objectives of the thesis were to:

- Examine the relationship between agricultural production growth and export growth.
- Investigate the direction of the relationship between production and exports.
- Analyze the long-run relationships among the relevant economic variables.
- Examine short-run adjustments following deviations from long-run equilibrium.
- Derive evidence relevant to agricultural export development and trade policy.

## Data and Scope

The thesis uses annual data for Iran covering the period 1990–2019 and examines selected agricultural export products.

The product-level analysis focuses on the relationship between production performance and export performance. The exact product list and variable definitions should be documented alongside the relevant research materials.

## Econometric Methods

The empirical analysis uses several econometric approaches, including:

- Granger causality analysis
- Cointegration analysis
- Error Correction Model (ECM)
- Ordinary Least Squares (OLS)

These methods are used to examine the direction of relationships, long-run associations, and short-run adjustment dynamics.

## Research Relevance

Understanding the relationship between agricultural production and exports can help inform discussions of export capacity, agricultural market development, and international trade policy.

The research is relevant to agricultural economics, international agricultural trade, and quantitative economic analysis.
